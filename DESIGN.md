## Overview

The portfolio is the Magic UI startup template with Diego’s work in it. A hiring manager lands on a black page, reads one gradient sentence, and sees both running apps fade into the field.

## Colors

Black `#000000` is the page. Paper `#fafafa` is the hero button. White `#ffffff` starts the headline gradient. Ink `#171717` is the text on that button and the selected tab. Line `#262626` is the pill border. Border `#404040` is rules, frames, and the header button. Hover `#525252` is the header button hover. Dim `#737373` is captions and the footer note. Muted `#a3a3a3` is body copy. Silver `#d4d4d4` is the pill. Word `#e5e5e5` is the marquee. Glow `#e8b86d` sits behind the hero image.

## Typography

Inter. The headline is a fluid 40–96px, weight 500, tracking -0.05em, clipped to a white-to-transparent gradient. Section titles are a fluid 36–60px, weight 700. Body is 15px. UI labels are 13px and 14px.

## Layout

The column is 1280px. The header is fixed, 56px, blurred. The hero is centered: pill, gradient sentence, one paragraph, a white Write button, then the device shot with a warm glow and a fade into black. A marquee of the real stack follows. Case studies keep their tabs, screenshots, and facts.

## Shapes

Hero and screenshot frames are 12px. The hero button is 8px. The header button is 6px. Pills and tabs are fully round. The close button is a 32px pill.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do keep the black field, the gradient sentence, and the fading screenshot. Do use Diego’s real work, not invented customers or prices.

Do not claim Google, Microsoft, or other companies as clients. Do not add a pricing table.
